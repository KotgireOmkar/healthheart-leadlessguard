# HealthHeart™ | LeadlessGuard™ Future Development Roadmap
**Classification:** `FUTURE CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT`

---

## 1. Roadmap Milestones

### Phase 1: Dual-Chamber Leadless Synchronization
- Emulation of communicating intracardiac leadless capsules (Atrial Leadless + Ventricular Leadless) via conductive intracardiac communication (i-BLE).

### Phase 2: Edge ML Arrhythmia Detection
- Deployment of quantized tinyML transformer models directly into virtual microcontroller firmware for sub-milliwatt arrhythmia classification.

### Phase 3: Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Integration
- Interfacing with physical hardware signal generators and physiological phantom testbeds via standardized IEEE 11073 medical device communication protocols.
