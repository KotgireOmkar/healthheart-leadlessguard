# HealthHeart™ | LeadlessGuard™ Research Limitations Document
**Classification:** `RESEARCH ONLY` / `SIMULATION`

---

## 1. Scope & Research Limitations
- **Synthetic Physiological Models:** Biosignals (ECG, PPG, SpO2, BIA) are synthesized via Gaussian mixtures and pulsatile mathematical approximations for engineering and cybersecurity testing; they are not physiological measurements from a live patient.
- **Simulated Radio Layer:** BLE telemetry is emulated via WebSockets and local REST endpoints rather than physical 2.4 GHz radio transceivers.
- **Regulatory Status:** This software platform is an educational and engineering concept prototype; it does not claim FDA 510(k), CE Mark, or regulatory clinical validation.
